## Abstract

A career in computer science is shaped as much by where you end up as by what you work on — and, above all, by the people you encounter along the way.

This talk takes a personal tour through the places that have formed the work: from Saarbrücken to Passau to Munich, through workshops and reading groups and chance conversations in conference corridors. At each stop, certain problems revealed themselves, certain collaborations took root, and certain ideas grew into something worth publishing — or at least worth arguing about over dinner.

The science will make a brief appearance. The people will feature more prominently.
